# Connect and support

Connect now stores consultation requests in Firestore. Individual and group sessions share one workflow: customer request → support review → confirmed date, advisor and existing meeting link → completed, declined or cancelled. Times display in the Philippines timezone. Confirmation enables a validated Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams link and a downloadable calendar event. It does not create an external meeting or send calendar/email invitations. Group attendees receive the meeting link through the requesting customer; no external participant invitation service is connected.

The support administration page has a Consultation desk for reviewing requests, confirming or changing schedules, declining with a reason, and recording completion. Customer actions cannot confirm meetings. All writes use callable functions with current verified identity, role checks, optimistic revisions, atomic history, idempotent creation and a five-request daily limit. Customers read their own records; verified support admins read the desk. Deploy consultationCreate, consultationUpdate and the Firestore rules with the frontend before enabling live use. Support administrators use the existing admin custom claim or configured SUPPORT_ADMIN_EMAILS through syncSupportAdmin. Company admins are not automatically support admins.

The old timed “LIVE” conference and simulated camera controls are removed. Existing provider meeting links handle audio, video and screen sharing. The site does not claim advisor online presence or seed conversations with fake human messages. The existing automatic acknowledgment is clearly labeled and no longer promises a 24-hour response.

Chat writes a message and preview atomically, retains unsent drafts on errors, prevents a draft following a different recipient, and marks a conversation read only while it is displayed. Saved support replies are not reported as unsent when optional push notifications fail. Current support permissions are checked on the server; claim synchronization preserves other claims. Realtime Database rules deny sender impersonation and customer edits/deletions of existing messages, and restrict creation to verified owners. Deploy database.rules.json with these changes.

Validation: nine consultation model tests, six callable authorization/state tests, and four support callable tests use local deterministic or in-memory service doubles. They make no customer-data writes and send no messages. Firebase Rules emulator/integration testing and provider-specific live meeting tests are still required before production release; no deployment was performed in this task.
